A launch date has a way of making everyone skip steps. This is the list I actually run through before a site goes live, whether it's a brand-new build or a migration from an old platform. It's saved me more than once from a broken redirect or a missing meta description going unnoticed until someone else found it first.

### Design and user experience

- Confirm the site is fully responsive and tested on real phones, not just a resized browser window
- Run through the site as if you're a first-time visitor and note anywhere navigation isn't obvious

### Content and SEO basics

- Review every page for accuracy. Nothing worse than launching with outdated pricing or a dead team member's bio still up
- Check that every page has a unique meta title and description, not a default placeholder

### Technical SEO

- Set up 301 redirects for any URL that's changing, especially on a migration
- Submit your XML sitemap, verify canonical tags, and validate JSON-LD structured data across templates
- Connect GA4 and Search Console before launch, not after, so day-one data isn't lost
- Confirm robots.txt isn't accidentally blocking search engines from crawling the new site

### Performance

- Compress images and confirm modern formats are in use
- Minify CSS and JavaScript, and confirm browser caching is actually configured

### Security

- Install and confirm the SSL certificate is active sitewide, not just on the homepage
- Update every plugin, theme and dependency to its current version before launch

### Legal

- Confirm a privacy policy and terms of service are live and linked from the footer
- Check compliance requirements relevant to your audience: GDPR, CCPA, or others depending on where visitors are located

### Cross-browser and device testing

- Test in Chrome, Firefox and Safari at minimum, on both desktop and mobile

### Social and sharing

- Confirm social links point to the right accounts and open properly
- Check that share previews (Open Graph tags) show the right title, image and description when a page gets shared

### Final QA pass

- Click every form, every CTA and every internal link once, all the way through
- Have someone who didn't build the site try to break it. They will find something you missed

### After launch

- Watch traffic and behavior closely for the first week
- Keep the redirect map handy in case something surfaces that wasn't caught beforehand
