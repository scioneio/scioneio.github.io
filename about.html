My story

A little more about me

I'm a lead web developer and organic growth engineer at Market Vantage, where I lead web builds, SEO, GEO/AEO, GA4, and channel attribution forensics for B2B clients. That includes leading Acclaro's Webflow rebuild, building custom HubSpot CMS themes, and creating internal AI workflows like a persona generator to keep content aligned to the right audience. This site is where I share the thinking behind my work across web development, organic visibility, and AI systems.

My background started in electronics. I studied electronics in the engineering cluster at Greater Lawrence Technical School, graduated valedictorian in 2008, worked hands-on as a technician and reclaimed inventory manager at Pinnacle Electronics, and started teaching Blender 3D workshops alongside MIT undergraduates through Build-It-Yourself as a teenager. After earning Dean's List honors with a 3.5+ GPA at UMass Lowell on the UMASS Scholars Scholarship, I chose as a self-learner to go straight into industry.

In 2009, I founded Image Lucidity, a Lowell multimedia studio I ran for over a decade building brand identities, websites, custom-coded tools, and 3D animation for clients and agency partners while continuing as Build-It-Yourself's contract webmaster and multimedia instructor. From 2018 to 2020, I stepped into a full-time operations manager role at Build-It-Yourself, rebuilding Build-It-Blocks and co-hosting sessions at MIT's Universal Village in 2018 and 2020 alongside founder John Galinato, in addition to speaking with Boston University's U-Design program. I joined Market Vantage full-time in 2021, and each chapter since electronics has reinforced the same habit: looking at a business as a connected system and finding the small changes that move the whole thing.
