Experience

I studied electronics in the engineering cluster at Greater Lawrence Technical School, graduated valedictorian in 2008, worked hands-on in electronics at Pinnacle Electronics, and as a teenager was already teaching Blender 3D workshops alongside MIT undergraduates through Build-It-Yourself. After earning Dean's List honors with a 3.5+ GPA at UMass Lowell on the UMASS Scholars Scholarship, I chose as a self-learner to move straight into industry.

In 2009, I founded Image Lucidity, a Lowell multimedia studio I ran for over a decade building brand, web, and 3D work for clients and agency partners while continuing as Build-It-Yourself's webmaster and multimedia instructor. From 2018 to 2020, I stepped into a full-time operations manager role at Build-It-Yourself and co-hosted sessions at MIT's Universal Village alongside founder John Galinato. Since 2021, I've been at Market Vantage as lead web developer and organic growth engineer.
